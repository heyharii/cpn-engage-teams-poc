# Product Scope

## Product Shape

The product should feel like one ecosystem, not a set of disconnected apps.

### Employee-Facing Surfaces

1. `CPN Engage App`
2. `Community Feed`

### Internal Surface

1. `Admin Console`

## In Scope Features

### Employee App

- Home dashboard
- Learning Journey
- Challenge and quiz
- Reflection submission
- Recognition submission
- Personal progress
- Leaderboard summary

### Community Feed

- Recognition feed
- Weekly leaderboard posts
- Announcement posts
- Comment and reaction-ready feed layout

### Feed Surfaces To Validate

- Teams private notifications or chat
- Custom community feed tab
- Native Teams Communities via Viva Engage

### Admin Console

- Campaign creation
- Content scheduling
- Approval and moderation
- Audience targeting
- Analytics dashboard
- Publish destination selection

### Notifications

- Assigned learning reminder
- Challenge reminder
- Recognition approval request
- Weekly leaderboard announcement

## POC Success Criteria

- All private flows are interactive and save state.
- Recognition goes through a real approval flow.
- Approved content appears in the public feed.
- Points affect leaderboard ranking.
- Admins can create and publish content without database edits.
- Analytics show activity from demo journeys.

## Not Required For First POC

- Native Praise card parity
- Full HRIS sync
- Rich video moderation pipeline
- Production-grade retention policy engine
- Deep AI personalization
