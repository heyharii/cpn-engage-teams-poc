# Risk Register

## High Risk

### Native Communities Publishing

- uses legacy Viva Engage / Yammer APIs
- delegated-only access
- tenant enablement may block testing

### Teams Tenant Prerequisites

- app sideloading or registration may require admin support
- test users and Communities access must exist

## Medium Risk

### Media Handling

- images are manageable for POC
- video should remain out of first build unless required

### Analytics Fidelity

- POC metrics should remain event-summary based
- avoid deep reporting scope in first pass

## Low Risk

### Custom Feed

- fully controlled by our stack
- suitable as source of truth for public product behavior
